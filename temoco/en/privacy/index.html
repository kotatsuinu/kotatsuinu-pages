temoco Privacy Policy

Operated by: Kotatsuinu
Contact: kotatsuinu.creator@gmail.com

Effective date: September 27, 2026

About this app

temoco is an app that records audio on Apple Watch or iPhone and delivers the recorded files to a storage location you choose. temoco itself does not transcribe or analyze the content of your recordings.

What we do not collect

temoco does not use any of the following:

  • Analytics tools
  • Advertising
  • A custom server operated by the developer (Kotatsuinu)

The developer never receives or stores your recorded audio, the content of your recordings, or your credentials for the storage service you use.

Where your recordings go

Your recorded audio files are sent directly to the storage location you chose in advance, without passing through any server operated by the developer (the developer does not operate a server at all).

If you choose Dropbox: temoco writes only to a folder created specifically for temoco. temoco cannot access any other files or folders in your Dropbox.

If you choose iCloud Drive: files are saved directly from your device to the "temoco" folder inside your iCloud Drive.

You choose either Dropbox or iCloud Drive as your single storage destination. temoco does not send recordings to both at the same time.

Where your login information is stored

When you connect Dropbox, your login credentials (access token) are stored in your device's Keychain. You sign in on Dropbox's own screen, so temoco never receives your Dropbox password.

How recordings are handled on your device

Recordings that were successfully delivered are automatically removed from your device after a period of time. Recordings that could not be delivered remain on your device until delivery succeeds.

Purpose of microphone use

temoco uses the microphone only while a recording is in progress, from when you start it until it stops. Its only purpose is to record a voice memo and deliver it to the storage location you chose. temoco does not perform speech recognition or transcription on your recordings.

Information sent when you use the bug-report button

Tapping the bug-report button in Settings opens a pre-filled email draft, which you can review and edit before sending. It automatically includes:

  • App version
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • Whether an Apple Watch is paired, and its version
  • Which storage location you have selected (Dropbox / iCloud Drive)
  • Number of recordings not yet delivered
  • The reason and time of the last failed delivery

This report never includes your recordings, their content, or your account name/credentials for the storage service.

Contact emails

Anything you send us by email (message body, reply-to address, etc.) is used only to respond to your inquiry. We do not use it for any other purpose and do not share it with third parties.

Children's use

temoco does not collect personal information from anyone, so it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the update and its date on this page.

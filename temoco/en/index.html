temoco Support

temoco is an app that records audio on your Apple Watch or iPhone and automatically delivers it to a storage location you choose (Dropbox or iCloud Drive). temoco does not transcribe or analyze the content of your recordings — it is a simple tool that just gets your recording from "record" to "delivered," for you to process with your own transcription or organizing workflow.

Free early-access release / by Kotatsuinu

How to use it

Recording on iPhone

  1. Open the app
  2. Tap the large record button once to start, tap again to stop
  3. Recording continues even if you lock the screen or switch to another app

Recording on Apple Watch

  1. Open the app on your Watch (from the app list, or from a complication on your watch face if you've added one).
  2. Tap the round button in the center of the screen to start recording. On models that support the Double Tap gesture (Apple Watch Series 9, Ultra 2 and later), you can also start without touching the screen by quickly tapping your index finger and thumb together twice.
  3. While recording, the button's display expands and shows the elapsed time.
  4. To stop, tap the round button again or use Double Tap. Recording is saved automatically when you stop.
  5. If you start a recording by mistake, tap the "×" in the top-left corner. You'll be asked "Discard without saving?" — choosing "Discard" deletes it without saving or sending it anywhere.
  6. Recordings stopped in under 3 seconds, or recordings that contain no voice at all, are not saved automatically (to guard against accidental taps).
  7. Even if you don't touch anything, recording stops and saves automatically after 1 minute of silence, or after 30 minutes.

Note: Turning on "Start recording immediately" in Settings makes recording begin the instant you open the app on your Watch (this is off by default).

Connecting a storage location

If you choose Dropbox
temoco connects using a folder created specifically for temoco. It cannot access any other files in your Dropbox. Sign in to Dropbox from the Settings screen.

If you choose iCloud Drive
If you are signed into iCloud and have iCloud Drive turned on, simply select it in the Settings screen. Your recordings are saved to a "temoco" folder inside iCloud Drive.

You choose one destination at a time — temoco does not send to both simultaneously. You can still record before choosing a destination; recordings queue on your device and are sent in order once you choose one.

FAQ

Q. Where does my recorded data go?
A. Directly to the storage location you chose (your Dropbox temoco folder, or the "temoco" folder in your iCloud Drive). It never passes through a developer-operated server, and the developer (Kotatsuinu) never sees the content of your recordings.

Q. What happens when the app becomes paid?
A. If you got the app during the free early-access period, you keep using it for free after it becomes paid — no additional purchase needed.

Q. Do I need an Apple Watch?
A. No. You can record and deliver recordings using only your iPhone.

Q. My recording isn't reaching my storage location.
A. Please check:

  • That your device is connected to the internet
  • If using Dropbox: that the Dropbox connection hasn't been disconnected (sign in again from Settings)
  • If using iCloud Drive: that you're signed into iCloud, iCloud Drive is on, and you have enough storage space
  • The recordings list screen, which shows why recordings couldn't be sent

Q. Can I change my storage location later?
A. Yes, from the Settings screen. Any recordings not yet sent will be delivered to the newly selected location.

Q. What languages are supported?
A. Japanese and English. The app follows your device's language setting automatically.

Q. Can I start recording with one tap, without opening the app?
A. There's no one-tap shortcut (such as Control Center). You open the app and tap the record button. On Apple Watch, you can turn on a setting so recording starts the moment you open temoco.

Bug reports & contact
